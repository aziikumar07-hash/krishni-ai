✦ Features

Everything You Need to Grow Smarter

Powerful AI tools designed specifically for Indian farming conditions and real field decisions.

🤖

AI Crop Advisory Live

Trained on 10+ years of IMD weather data and ISRO satellite imagery. Recommends optimal sowing windows, irrigation schedules, and harvest timing for rice, wheat, cotton, and vegetables across 20+ Indian districts.

🔬

Disease Detection 94% Accuracy

Built on a dataset of 50,000+ labelled crop images collected with ICAR-KVK partners. Detects early blight, late blight, leaf curl, and powdery mildew from a simple phone photo — no internet required for diagnosis.

⛅

Weather-Based Alerts Coming Soon

Pulls hyperlocal forecasts from the Indian Meteorological Department API. Sends WhatsApp alerts for unseasonal rain, heatwaves, and pest outbreaks — delivered in Hindi, Marathi, Telugu, and English.

📊

Yield Prediction Coming Soon

Uses NDVI indices from Sentinel-2 satellite data combined with soil pH, NPK levels, and historical yield patterns from your region. Generate harvest estimates 6–8 weeks before harvest to plan pricing and storage.
